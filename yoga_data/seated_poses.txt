SEATED YOGA POSES

1. DANDASANA (Staff Pose)
Description: The foundation of all seated poses. Sit with legs extended straight in front.
Instructions:
- Sit with legs extended straight in front
- Press thighs and calves into the floor
- Flex feet, toes pointing up
- Place hands beside hips, fingers pointing forward
- Sit up tall, lengthening spine
- Roll shoulders back and down
Benefits: Strengthens back, stretches chest and shoulders, improves posture
Duration: Hold for 30-60 seconds
Common Mistakes: Rounding the lower back, letting legs roll out
Modifications: Sit on a folded blanket to tilt pelvis forward

2. PASCHIMOTTANASANA (Seated Forward Bend)
Description: A deep forward fold that stretches the entire back of the body.
Instructions:
- Begin in Dandasana
- Inhale and lengthen spine
- Exhale and hinge forward from hips (not waist)
- Reach for feet, ankles or shins
- Keep spine long, avoid rounding upper back
- Let head hang heavy or rest on legs
Benefits: Stretches hamstrings, spine and lower back, calms nervous system
Duration: Hold for 1-3 minutes
Common Mistakes: Rounding spine, pulling with arms, bending knees excessively
Modifications: Use a strap around feet, sit on blanket, bend knees slightly

3. BADDHA KONASANA (Butterfly / Bound Angle Pose)
Description: A hip opener that stretches inner thighs and groin.
Instructions:
- Sit with soles of feet together, knees falling out to sides
- Hold feet or ankles with both hands
- Sit tall, lengthening spine
- Gently press knees toward floor (do not force)
- Option to fold forward over feet
Benefits: Opens hips and groin, stretches inner thighs, stimulates digestion
Duration: Hold for 1-3 minutes
Common Mistakes: Forcing knees down, rounding back, collapsing forward
Modifications: Sit on blanket, place blocks under knees for support

4. ARDHA MATSYENDRASANA (Half Lord of the Fishes / Seated Twist)
Description: A spinal twist that improves mobility and detoxifies the body.
Instructions:
- Sit with legs extended, bend right knee and cross right foot over left leg
- Place right foot flat on floor outside left knee
- Bend left knee, tuck left foot near right hip (or keep extended)
- Inhale and lengthen spine
- Exhale and twist to the right
- Place right hand behind you, left elbow outside right knee
- Gaze over right shoulder
Benefits: Improves spinal mobility, stretches shoulders and hips, aids digestion
Duration: Hold for 30-60 seconds each side
Common Mistakes: Collapsing spine, twisting from neck only, holding breath
Modifications: Keep bottom leg extended, sit on blanket

5. SUKHASANA (Easy Pose)
Description: A comfortable cross-legged sitting position for meditation and breathing.
Instructions:
- Sit cross-legged, each foot below opposite knee
- Sit on sitting bones, not tailbone
- Rest hands on knees, palms up or down
- Lengthen spine, relax shoulders
- Soften face and jaw
Benefits: Opens hips, lengthens spine, calms mind, good for meditation
Duration: As long as comfortable
Common Mistakes: Rounding lower back, knees higher than hips
Modifications: Sit on blanket or block to elevate hips
