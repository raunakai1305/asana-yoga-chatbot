FLOOR / SUPINE YOGA POSES

1. BALASANA (Child's Pose)
Description: A resting pose that gently stretches the back and calms the mind.
Instructions:
- Kneel on the floor, big toes touching, knees hip-width apart
- Exhale and fold torso between thighs
- Extend arms forward or rest alongside body
- Rest forehead on the floor
- Relax completely, breathe deeply
Benefits: Releases back tension, calms nervous system, stretches hips and thighs
Duration: Hold for 1-3 minutes or as needed for rest
Common Mistakes: Not fully relaxing, holding tension in shoulders
Modifications: Place blanket between thighs and calves, use block under forehead

2. BHUJANGASANA (Cobra Pose)
Description: A gentle backbend that opens the chest and strengthens the spine.
Instructions:
- Lie face down, legs extended, tops of feet on floor
- Place hands under shoulders, elbows close to body
- Press tops of feet and thighs into floor
- Inhale and slowly lift chest off floor
- Keep elbows slightly bent, shoulders away from ears
- Gaze forward or slightly upward
Benefits: Strengthens spine, opens chest and lungs, stimulates abdominal organs
Duration: Hold for 15-30 seconds
Common Mistakes: Straightening arms too much, crunching neck, lifting hips
Modifications: Keep forearms on floor (Sphinx pose) for less intensity

3. SETU BANDHA SARVANGASANA (Bridge Pose)
Description: A backbend that strengthens the back and opens the chest.
Instructions:
- Lie on back, knees bent, feet hip-width apart and flat on floor
- Arms alongside body, palms down
- Press feet into floor and lift hips toward ceiling
- Clasp hands under back, press arms into floor
- Keep thighs parallel, knees over ankles
- Hold for several breaths
Benefits: Strengthens back, glutes and hamstrings, opens chest, reduces anxiety
Duration: Hold for 30-60 seconds
Common Mistakes: Knees splaying out, neck straining, lower back crunching
Modifications: Place block under sacrum for supported bridge

4. SAVASANA (Corpse Pose)
Description: The final relaxation pose. Considered the most important pose in yoga.
Instructions:
- Lie flat on back, legs extended naturally apart
- Arms at sides, palms facing up, slightly away from body
- Close eyes, relax every part of the body
- Let feet fall open, release all muscle tension
- Breathe naturally, let mind be still
Benefits: Relaxes entire body, reduces stress, integrates the benefits of practice
Duration: 5-10 minutes at end of practice
Common Mistakes: Skipping or shortening savasana, fidgeting, falling asleep
Modifications: Place blanket under knees for lower back comfort, eye pillow for relaxation

5. SUPTA MATSYENDRASANA (Supine Spinal Twist)
Description: A gentle lying twist that releases the spine and lower back.
Instructions:
- Lie on back, draw right knee to chest
- Let right knee fall across body to the left
- Extend right arm out to the right, gaze right
- Keep both shoulders grounded
- Left hand can rest on right knee
Benefits: Releases spine and lower back, stretches outer hips, aids digestion
Duration: Hold for 1-2 minutes each side
Common Mistakes: Lifting opposite shoulder off floor, forcing knee to floor
Modifications: Place block or blanket under knee for support
