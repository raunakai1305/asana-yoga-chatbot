STANDING YOGA POSES

1. TADASANA (Mountain Pose)
Description: The foundation of all standing poses. Stand with feet together, arms at sides, spine tall.
Instructions:
- Stand with big toes touching, heels slightly apart
- Distribute weight evenly across both feet
- Engage thigh muscles and lift kneecaps
- Lengthen tailbone toward the floor
- Roll shoulders back and down
- Extend arms alongside body, palms facing forward
- Gaze forward, chin parallel to floor
Benefits: Improves posture, strengthens thighs and ankles, increases body awareness
Duration: Hold for 30 seconds to 1 minute
Common Mistakes: Locking knees, arching lower back, tensing shoulders
Modifications: Stand with back against wall for balance support

2. VIRABHADRASANA I (Warrior I)
Description: A powerful standing pose that builds strength and focus.
Instructions:
- Start in Tadasana, step left foot back 3-4 feet
- Turn left foot out 45 degrees
- Bend right knee directly over right ankle
- Square hips toward the front of the mat
- Raise arms overhead, palms facing each other
- Draw shoulder blades down the back
- Gaze up toward hands or forward
Benefits: Strengthens legs, opens hips and chest, builds stamina and focus
Duration: Hold for 30-60 seconds each side
Common Mistakes: Front knee collapsing inward, back heel lifting, overarching lower back
Modifications: Shorten stance for tight hips, keep hands on hips if shoulder issues

3. VIRABHADRASANA II (Warrior II)
Description: A wide-legged standing pose that opens the hips and builds leg strength.
Instructions:
- Stand with feet 3-4 feet apart
- Turn right foot out 90 degrees, left foot in slightly
- Bend right knee over right ankle
- Extend arms parallel to floor, palms down
- Gaze over right fingertips
- Keep torso upright, not leaning forward
Benefits: Strengthens legs and core, opens hips and chest, improves concentration
Duration: Hold for 30-60 seconds each side
Common Mistakes: Front knee falling inward, torso leaning forward, arms drooping
Modifications: Use a chair for support, reduce depth of lunge

4. TRIKONASANA (Triangle Pose)
Description: A lateral stretch that lengthens the sides of the body.
Instructions:
- Stand with feet 3-4 feet apart
- Turn right foot out 90 degrees, left foot in slightly
- Extend arms to the sides at shoulder height
- Reach right hand toward right shin, ankle or floor
- Extend left arm toward ceiling
- Keep both legs straight, kneecaps lifted
- Gaze up at left hand or forward
Benefits: Stretches hips, groins, hamstrings, opens chest, strengthens legs
Duration: Hold for 30-60 seconds each side
Common Mistakes: Rounding the spine, collapsing chest toward floor
Modifications: Use a block under bottom hand, keep slight bend in front knee

5. VRKSASANA (Tree Pose)
Description: A balancing pose that builds focus and stability.
Instructions:
- Begin in Tadasana, shift weight to left foot
- Place right foot on inner left thigh or calf (not knee)
- Press foot and thigh against each other
- Bring hands to prayer position or raise overhead
- Fix gaze on a still point ahead
- Keep standing leg slightly bent
Benefits: Improves balance and concentration, strengthens legs and core, opens hips
Duration: Hold for 30-60 seconds each side
Common Mistakes: Placing foot on knee joint, gripping toes, holding breath
Modifications: Keep toes on floor, use wall for balance support
