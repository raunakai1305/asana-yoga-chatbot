INVERSIONS AND BALANCE POSES

1. ADHO MUKHA SVANASANA (Downward Facing Dog)
Description: One of the most recognized yoga poses. A mild inversion and full body stretch.
Instructions:
- Start on hands and knees, wrists under shoulders, knees under hips
- Tuck toes, lift knees off floor
- Straighten legs and lift hips toward ceiling
- Form an inverted V shape with body
- Press hands firmly into floor, spread fingers wide
- Relax head between arms, gaze toward navel
- Heels reach toward floor (need not touch)
Benefits: Stretches hamstrings, calves, shoulders, strengthens arms and legs, energizes body
Duration: Hold for 1-3 minutes
Common Mistakes: Rounding upper back, locking elbows, weight in wrists only
Modifications: Bend knees slightly, use blocks under hands

2. SIRSASANA (Headstand)
Description: The king of yoga poses. A full inversion that requires strength and balance.
Instructions:
- Kneel and interlace fingers, place forearms on floor
- Place crown of head on floor, cradled by hands
- Straighten legs and walk feet toward head
- Lift one knee then the other to chest
- Slowly extend legs straight up
- Engage core throughout, breathe steadily
Benefits: Increases circulation, strengthens shoulders and core, improves focus and balance
Duration: Begin with 10-30 seconds, build up gradually
Common Mistakes: Putting too much weight on head, collapsing shoulders, kicking up forcefully
Modifications: Practice against wall, work on dolphin pose first as preparation
Contraindications: High blood pressure, neck injuries, glaucoma, pregnancy

3. SARVANGASANA (Shoulder Stand)
Description: The queen of yoga poses. A supported inversion.
Instructions:
- Lie on back, lift legs and hips off floor
- Support lower back with hands, elbows on floor
- Extend legs straight up toward ceiling
- Keep weight on shoulders and upper arms, not neck
- Chin draws toward chest
Benefits: Stimulates thyroid, improves circulation, calms nervous system
Duration: Hold for 1-5 minutes
Common Mistakes: Putting weight on neck, not supporting lower back
Modifications: Use folded blankets under shoulders, practice legs-up-the-wall instead
Contraindications: Neck injuries, high blood pressure, during menstruation

4. BAKASANA (Crow Pose)
Description: An arm balance that builds strength and concentration.
Instructions:
- Squat with feet together, knees wide
- Place hands shoulder-width on floor, spread fingers
- Bend elbows slightly, place knees on back of upper arms
- Lean weight forward onto hands
- Lift one foot then both off floor
- Round back, draw belly in, gaze forward
Benefits: Strengthens wrists, arms and core, improves balance and focus
Duration: Hold for 10-30 seconds
Common Mistakes: Fear of falling forward, not engaging core, straight arms
Modifications: Place block under feet, practice with feet on block

5. NATARAJASANA (Dancer's Pose)
Description: A beautiful standing balance that combines strength, flexibility and grace.
Instructions:
- Stand in Tadasana, shift weight to left foot
- Bend right knee, reach back and hold right ankle with right hand
- Lift right leg behind you, pressing foot into hand
- Extend left arm forward and up
- Keep hips square, torso upright
- Fix gaze on a still point
Benefits: Strengthens legs and core, opens chest and shoulders, improves balance
Duration: Hold for 20-30 seconds each side
Common Mistakes: Hips twisting open, collapsing in standing leg
Modifications: Hold foot with strap, practice near wall
